## Two different three-step sequences

**C-Level AI** presents Workshop → Scorecard → Discovery Session as a free executive entry. **3-Step ROI Accelerator** presents Audit → Build & Deploy → Validate as an implementation engagement. These should not be combined into one funnel without an explicit business decision: they describe different commitments and outcomes. [Sources: homepage](https://www.found42.com/), [introductory article](https://www.found42.com/blog/blog-post-title-two-cep4t).

The Accelerator article sets out an audit in week one, implementation in weeks two through four, and validation by day 60. Its framework name is not an exposed methodology: assessment questions, workflow selection criteria, baselines, calculation rules, and guarantee terms were not supplied in the inspected article. This remains a published offer description, not a confirmed current contract.

## Audience drift

The homepage targets C-level executives; its search/social description targets startups. ScoreApp targets SMB service businesses. The introduction narrows further to consulting firms below 1,000 employees, while the growth-platform article discusses professional services more broadly and the homepage's third service targets SaaS. These are live differences, not proof that one segment has formally replaced another. [Sources: homepage](https://www.found42.com/), [assessment](https://found42.scoreapp.com/), [introduction](https://www.found42.com/blog/blog-post-title-two-cep4t), [growth platforms](https://www.found42.com/blog/blog-post-title-three-ecr97).

The repository's business/PE direction is a redesign objective. No dedicated PE engagement offer, portfolio-company operating model, or verified PE outcome was established by the inspected pages. A fictional acquisition exercise belongs to Learning, not client proof.

## Recommendations and unresolved decisions

Use the three service names as a starting taxonomy, with plain descriptions of who each is for and what work it covers. Confirm whether the Growth Platform category remains broad or is intentionally narrowed to B2B SaaS. Treat the old Accelerator as a historical published offer until its status and terms are approved. Resolve these commercial questions before rewriting inquiry choices or retaining guarantees.

A future service page should distinguish the problem, work included, customer participation, deliverables, and evidence. The inspected site does not establish a standard engagement length, current package price, guaranteed outcome, or customer qualification rule that can safely be generalized across all three services.
