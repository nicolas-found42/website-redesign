# Services

This context defines Found42's tailored offerings and the public evidence used to describe them.

## Language

**AI Empowerment Training**:
Found42's training service for helping business users apply AI to their own work and develop an AI-first team culture.

**AI-Powered Automation**:
Found42's service for identifying and automating business workflows, including work spanning functions and systems.
_Avoid_: AI Empowerment Training when the work is cross-functional implementation

**AI Product Differentiation for B2B SaaS**:
Found42's service offering focused on AI-based differentiation for business-to-business software products.

**AI Growth Platform**:
Found42's broader concept of turning a business's existing intellectual property and know-how into AI-enabled products, services, or new revenue streams.
_Avoid_: Exact synonym for AI Product Differentiation for B2B SaaS

**AI Insourcing**:
Found42's term for bringing previously outsourced functions in-house through employees using AI agents and workflows.

**3-Step ROI Accelerator**:
The Audit → Build & Deploy → Validate engagement described in Found42's introductory article, distinct from the C-Level AI learning and discovery sequence.

**Found42 AI Framework**:
The named approach associated with workflow prioritization in Found42's introductory article; the name alone does not specify its methodology.
