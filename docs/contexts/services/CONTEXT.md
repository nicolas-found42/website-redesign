# Services

This context defines Found42's tailored offerings and the public evidence used to describe them.

## Language

**AI Empowerment Training**:
Found42's training service for helping people use AI in their work.

**AI-Powered Automation**:
Found42's service offering focused on applying AI to business automation.

**AI Product Differentiation for B2B SaaS**:
Found42's service offering focused on AI-based differentiation for business-to-business software products.

**Testimonial**:
An attributed statement from a named person about their experience. A testimonial is distinct from a documented case study or an endorsement by that person's employer.

**Service claim**:
A statement about an offering's capabilities or outcomes. Publication of a claim does not by itself establish supporting evidence.
