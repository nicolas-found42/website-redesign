# Learning

This context defines Found42's executive learning experience, its supporting tools, and practice exercises.

## Language

**C-Level AI**:
Found42's executive-focused learning and discovery offering, presented as a Workshop → Scorecard → Discovery Session sequence.
_Avoid_: A separate company, a software platform

**C-Level AI Workshop**:
The practical executive training experience covering communication personalization, advisory perspectives, and a mock business case. The site also calls it a mini-course.

**C-Level AI Toolkit**:
The collection of workshop materials, custom GPT links, and practice exercises supporting C-Level AI learning.
_Avoid_: Industry-Specific Prompt Packs, the full course enrollment experience

**Personalization GPT**:
The C-Level AI tool for developing a ChatGPT personalization configuration reflecting the executive's communication style and priorities.

**Board of Advisors GPT**:
The C-Level AI tool for exploring strategic questions through multiple simulated advisory perspectives.
_Avoid_: A board of human advisors, corporate governance board

**Negotiator GPT**:
The C-Level AI tool for practicing negotiation scenarios, anticipating responses, and preparing a negotiation approach.

**VossGPT**:
The separately linked negotiation assistant described in Found42's cross-cultural negotiation article as inspired by Chris Voss's methods.
_Avoid_: An established alias for the toolkit's Negotiator GPT

**M&A Mock Case Study**:
The fictional LexyAI–DocuWyze AI acquisition scenario used for executive practice, rather than a Found42 client transaction or performance result.

**Exercise personality profile**:
A fictional counterpart profile used to practice communication and negotiation within the mock acquisition scenario.

**90% Rule of AI Automation**:
The negotiation article's heuristic of using AI for preparation and handing the result to a human for contextual judgment and refinement.
_Avoid_: A measured accuracy rate or guaranteed automation percentage
