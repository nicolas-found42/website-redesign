# Resources

This context defines the useful material visitors can explore before requesting tailored help.

## Language

**Free resource**:
Material or an assessment offered without a monetary charge. Access may still require submitting personal or business details.
_Avoid_: Ungated resource when a form is required

**Public reading**:
Article content a visitor can read without submitting details.

**Resource access gate**:
A form or other requirement a visitor must satisfy to request or access a resource.

**Resource request**:
A submission seeking access to a free resource. It does not by itself establish a consultation inquiry or confirmed delivery.

**Resource fulfillment**:
The delivery or successful opening of the material or result requested by a visitor, beyond reaching its landing page or submitting its form.

**Industry-specific prompt packs**:
Found42's prompt offering organized by industry. The published inventory claims are distinct from an inspected inventory of delivered content.

**AI Failure Modes Playbook**:
Found42's guide offering about AI failure modes, requested through a resource access gate.

**AI Readiness Scorecard**:
Found42's assessment of a person's current AI use and readiness, with a personal report and PDF download. Name and role are optional; the report is available without contact details.
_Avoid_: Certification, audited ROI measurement

**Readiness stage**:
The scorecard's overall readiness band: Foundations, Ready to build or Ready to scale. The stage summarizes the overall score; it does not certify readiness.
_Avoid_: Certification, audited outcome

**Readiness report**:
The personal result of the AI Readiness Scorecard: an overall score and stage, five area results, safeguards, quick wins, recommendations and the answers supplied. Available on the page and as a downloadable PDF. Email delivery is a separate future fulfillment option.

**Depth Deficit**:
Found42's name for the gap between basic AI use and applying it to role-specific work deliverables.
