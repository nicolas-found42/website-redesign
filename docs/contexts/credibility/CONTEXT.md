# Credibility

This context defines the people, evidence, and brand representations visitors use to assess Found42's offerings.

## Language

**Found42**:
The business brand through which Found42 LLC presents AI learning, automation, and growth offerings.

**Founder biography**:
Found42's published account of Richard Achée's background and experience, distinct from independent verification of those statements.

**Testimonial**:
An attributed statement from a named person about their experience. A testimonial is distinct from a documented client case study or endorsement by that person's employer.

**Service claim**:
A statement about an offering's capabilities or outcomes. Publication of a claim does not by itself establish supporting evidence.

**Reported outcome**:
A result described by Found42 or a testimonial author, tied to that account and its circumstances rather than a general promise to future customers.

**Mock case**:
A fictional scenario used for instruction or practice, distinct from evidence of a completed customer engagement.
_Avoid_: Client case study, customer success story

**Brand asset**:
An identity element such as the Found42 logo, C-Level AI graphic, or founder portrait, distinct from a documented brand standard or usage license.
