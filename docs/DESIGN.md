# Design decisions

The eight-page preview uses a calm business-site voice around the **Working Drawings** system. [ADR 0007](adr/0007-approved-layout-with-deployed-drawings.md) governs the homepage order and the removal of the manual motion control; [ADR 0006](adr/0006-drawings-made-of-material.md) governs the drawings. [ADRs 0002–0005](adr/0002-working-system-drawing.md) retain the drawing, scene, narrow-layout and visual behavior they document. Earlier expressive and technical treatments remain in `preview/expressive/`, `preview/` and Git history as historical design evidence.

## Visual language

Space Grotesk carries display headings and the words inside drawings; Manrope carries the main reading and interface text. JetBrains Mono remains for small machine-like labels. The former printed grid, paper grain, section numbers and repeated figure captions have been removed from visitor-facing pages. Paper, ink and restrained red surfaces establish hierarchy through spacing, tone and a few purposeful dividers. Red identifies human direction in the drawings, then marks selected controls and important actions elsewhere.

Drawings use solid paper and card in the site's inks with charcoal outlines — no decorative dots, stripes, ruled texture or dashed stitching. Workshops remains the visual reference: people design at a whiteboard, practise at a keyboard, review together and leave with a reusable skill. The other five scenes now belong to real work settings: Workflows shows a customer engagement around source material and a reusable tool; Automations shows work packets passing between systems under human direction and review; executives use four distinct documents through their working day; Individual Contributors and Teams show four role-specific work artifacts whose function labels sit in open space along independent lanes passing through one neutral human-review icon; AI builders collaboratively design, test, investigate faults and anticipate failures in their own build. People perform reviews and decisions, while other stages can focus on documents or systems. [ADR 0011](adr/0011-owner-specific-work-settings.md) records the owner-confirmed direction and executive study's baseline detail level, superseding the earlier dominant silhouettes while preserving every label and connection, plus the audience marks and beats, from [ADR 0010](adr/0010-october-review-drawings.md).

The homepage opening carries no drawing; the plait sits beside industry copy. Landscape and portrait layouts are separately authored so words stay at least 13px on narrow screens. The executive's four sample lines, red “You decide” mark and illustrative disclosure remain. [ADR 0012](adr/0012-executive-output-documents.md) replaces the executive stick figures and desks with readable priority, agenda, follow-up and transcript-to-task document layouts. The drawings describe a possible customer journey, not a deployed customer system or measured outcome. [ADR 0006](adr/0006-drawings-made-of-material.md) records the drawing architecture and [ADR 0002](adr/0002-working-system-drawing.md) the engine they keep.

## Homepage journeys

The opening headline is “Train teams. Build useful skills. Automate the work.” Its supporting copy says Found42 trains people to create and use Claude Skills tailored to their roles and keeps judgment with the customer's team. **Talk to us** opens the inquiry dialog; **Explore free resources** opens the Resources page. An attributed participant quote sits beside the actions on a wide screen and below them on a narrow one; no space is held for a drawing. Fifteen locally bundled official organization marks follow the hero in a continuously rotating strip, in the owner-approved order from Google through Prelude Solutions. Reduced motion presents all fifteen in a still, wrapped row. Each name is announced once; hidden copies complete the visual loop. The numerical proof band remains removed.

**Find the work that sounds like yours** opens with "Whether you are an executive, an individual contributor, or an AI builder, we have workshops tailored to your role that will put Claude to work and save you 4-8 hours every week." It presents three audience articles — C-level executives, Individual Contributors and Teams, AI builders — beside a pinned scene on wide screens, with a pill rail that follows reading and jumps to an article. Below 960px each article carries its own scene and the rail sits above the articles. Separate links lead to C-Level AI, Customized Role-Based Training and AI Builders entries in the Services catalog. Without script or with reduced motion, every article and its complete still scene remains readable in order. The executive example is illustrative, not a client artifact or a live integration. [ADR 0009](adr/0009-executive-day-and-audience-sequence.md) records the current audience behavior.

**Three ways we help** sits on the paper ground. Workshops, Workflows and Automations remain readable articles with the deployed sticky or per-article drawings. Workflows builds Claude skills and plugins for everyday, time-consuming tasks; Automations connects end-to-end processes to CRM, productivity apps and systems of record, retaining human judgment. The redundant weekly briefing band was removed in #168. Five complete testimonials follow the services directly, and the founder strip follows them before **Not ready to talk? Start here** previews the native AI Readiness Scorecard and the public C-Level AI Toolkit.

The full Resources page owns the five-item inventory and its access and availability details. The scorecard runs in the browser and gives a readiness stage and next steps; its external ScoreApp report is a separate, gated experience. The Toolkit opens its public page. The published playbook opens its existing request route. The Skills Starter Library offers four original Claude skill packages as direct downloads. Strategic Advisor offers its existing skill package, setup instructions and verified Maven lesson, as approved by the owner; it makes no five-day course or enrollment promise. [Resource sources](resource-sources.md) records the original Drive material and the three readable Blog draft excerpts. No local resource form claims to send or fulfill a request.

Each homepage service article has a contextual **Talk to us about…** action. The Services page retains its engagement comparison, audience scenes and animated service sequence, then presents five training tracks, six formats, four beyond-training services, two no-charge sessions, biography, attributed quotes and a starting path. Contextual **Inquire** actions name the offering in the shared handoff. Their underlying links lead to the live form without script; with script, the dialog explains how to carry the offering name into that form. The live form uses its own interest vocabulary. An inquiry requests a conversation; it is not a booked meeting. Paid amounts and client-specific proposal terms are absent.

## Motion and interaction

On wide screens the service drawing stays beside articles as they scroll, following the article crossing the viewport's middle band. The choice rail jumps directly to an article; the latest choice remains authoritative while its jump settles. On narrow screens each article has its own portrait drawing and the rail remains available above the sequence. All article copy is present independently of the drawing. [ADR 0004](adr/0004-narrow-services-sequence.md) records the responsive interaction.

Drawings are assembled in stages — each piece placed, never drawn on — then rest as complete compositions; nothing travels along them and the drawings do not loop. The organization strip loops continuously except under reduced motion, as explicitly approved by the owner in #80. The operating system's reduced-motion setting settles entrances and transitions, stops pointer depth, and ends smooth scrolling. A preference change during a service jump lands on the chosen article and leaves a complete drawing. No content depends on an animation finishing. The visible manual pause/play control has been removed; audience and service pill rails support pointer and keyboard choices while preserving focus.

## Pages, handoffs and release

The September 16 migration established the seven routes; [the content sources](CONTENT-SOURCES.md) and [site map](SITE-MAP.md) record their provenance and structure. The September 23 revision changes copy, journeys and presentation across those routes while preserving base-aware static navigation, direct entry and no-script content. Public resources, learning offerings, services and inquiries remain separate concepts in the [context map](../CONTEXT-MAP.md).

The shared dialog explains what the existing live contact form asks and what happens after an inquiry. It never reports a local submission as sent. Service names and the form's interest labels are intentionally distinct; the external HubSpot fields, consent defaults and routing remain owner decisions. Resource requests likewise remain separate from consultations. External destinations and fulfillment need owner-approved verification before release. The preview retains noindex until the separate production launch decision.
