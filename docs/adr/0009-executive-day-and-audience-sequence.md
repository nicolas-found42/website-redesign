# The executive day, organization strip and audience sequence

**Status:** accepted (owner's second preview review, 2026-09-29, issue #80)

This decision follows #78's completed refinements and supersedes the conflicting
organization, audience-selection and executive-scene statements in
[ADR 0008](0008-text-led-opening-and-solid-drawings.md). It also replaces
[ADR 0003](0003-audience-scenes.md)'s rejection of an audience scroll sequence.

## The executive's day

The executive scene is a timeline of four cards: **Claude Daily Brief** →
**Meeting Brief** → **Meeting Debrief** → **Actions from Transcripts**.
A compact red **You decide** mark on the last card keeps the executive's decision
visible. The operating-problem object, tailored-skill capsule, return loop and
executive-direction clip and seal are removed. The caption and accessible
meaning identify the scene as an illustrative Claude example, not a client
result or a live calendar, email, CRM or meeting-transcript integration.

The drawings retain separate wide and narrow compositions, solid fills and
charcoal outlines, real text, part-based entrances and complete still states
from ADRs 0003, 0006 and 0008. The inventory is revised by hand.

## Organizations

The owner confirmed fifteen relationships, shown in this order: Google,
Edgescale AI, MB&A (Millsapps, Ballinger & Associates), Seidler Equity Partners
(SEP), PeakSpan Capital, Palladium Security, Marajá, Ruruka, Crown Point Advisory
Group, IDC, ParaVet.live, mobile.club, MINDSi Sports Performance, Mindsight
and Prelude Solutions. Issue #163 corrects the former MINDS-i Education
identity to the owner-linked Mindsight entity; the Sports Performance entry
remains independently approved.

Their official marks are bundled locally without redrawing, tracing or
recolouring. Dark-background marks use tiles and each mark is sized by perceived
size. Marks identify relationships; they are neither links nor endorsements.
A CSS strip moves continuously in one direction at every width, with a hidden
copy completing the loop. Assistive technology receives each name once.
Reduced motion shows every mark in a still, wrapped list. No script is needed.
The owner explicitly waived WCAG 2.2.2's pause control for this strip; there is
no pause button and hover does not pause it.

## Audiences

Home and Services apply [ADR 0004](0004-narrow-services-sequence.md)'s service
sequence to audiences. A wide screen pins the selected scene beside three
articles; reading through the viewport centre changes the scene, caption and
pressed pill. The rail jumps to an article and uses each audience's full name.
Below the services breakpoint, each article carries its own scene and the rail
sits above the articles. All articles retain their kicker, name, proposition,
three points and Services link. The equal cards, arrows and counter are gone.
The caption does not announce scrolling through a live region. Without script
or with reduced motion, all articles and their still scenes remain in order.

## Service vocabulary and preserved decisions

**Workflows** means custom Claude skills and plugins for everyday, high-value,
time-consuming tasks. **Automations** means end-to-end automations connected to
CRM, productivity apps and systems of record, with human judgment retained.
Shared copy and the Services glossary express this distinction on both pages.
The owner approved the integration claim; it is a commercial statement rather
than independently verified integration evidence.

The approved introductory sentence, weekly briefing refinements from #78,
text-led hero, industry braid, contributor and builder artwork, Workshops
scene, inquiry interests and generic workflow prose stay as decided before.
