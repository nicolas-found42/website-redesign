# Executive output documents

**Status:** accepted (October review follow-through, issue #164, 2026-10-09)

The reviewer found the executive stick figures distracting and its artifacts
unclear and generic. The executive scene now gives the four output documents
the full space previously shared with people, desks and secondary objects.
This supersedes the executive work-setting treatment in
[ADR 0011](0011-owner-specific-work-settings.md); the other scenes keep that
direction.

The Claude Daily Brief shows three ranked priorities with summaries and detail.
The Meeting Brief shows timed agenda blocks. The Meeting Debrief pairs follow-up
items with owner and calendar fields. Actions from Transcripts shows source
notes feeding an unchecked task list, with the separate red **You decide** mark
retaining the executive's decision. These are recognizable document structures,
not actual customer documents or live data. The existing four titles, sample
lines, illustrative disclosure, marks, beats and connections remain unchanged.
The accessible description and its inventory entry name the output structures.

Separate landscape and portrait compositions, real HTML labels, solid inks,
complete still states and part-based entrances remain as specified in
[ADR 0006](0006-drawings-made-of-material.md). The existing browser seam checks
output descriptions, the inventory and enlarged labels. Comparable desktop and
mobile captures in `artifacts/review-tickets/164/` record the visual change;
they establish the rendered layout, not measured visitor comprehension.
