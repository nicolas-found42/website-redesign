# Industries beyond Private Equity and B2B SaaS

The site's Industries navigation stays limited to the Private Equity and B2B
SaaS pages. It does not add a general "Operating businesses" framing, and
Industries is not removed from the header until more pages exist.

## Why this is out of scope

The request was raised as a positioning decision, not a defect: an owner of a
traditional small business finds no industry that fits them. The owner
declined to broaden the positioning (#48, closed as `wontfix` on 2026-09-29).
The published industry pages are the two listed in
[SITE-MAP](../docs/SITE-MAP.md).

The owner's fuller reasoning was not recorded when the issue was closed. Add it
here when it is written down, so this file can be judged on more than the
decision.
