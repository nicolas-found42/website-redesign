# Website feedback discovery questionnaire

**Purpose:** Give the developer clear direction on website content, homepage priorities, design changes, and requirements that may have been missed.

**From:** Nicolas  
**To:** The boss and project manager, each completing the same questionnaire independently  
**How your answers will be used:** Nicolas will compare your feedback, identify decisions that need alignment, and turn agreed direction into a prioritized list of website changes.

## Context

Please review the current website with our intended visitors and business goals in mind. Your perspective is needed to decide what we should say, what deserves space on the homepage, and how the site should look and feel. Focus on the direction you want the developer to implement; you do not need to propose a technical solution.

## How to answer

Allow about 15–20 minutes, including a browse through the website. Please return your answers before the next website review; a specific deadline is to be confirmed. Answer independently first so both perspectives are captured. Short bullets are fine, and partial answers or “I don't know” are useful. Flag uncertainty rather than skipping a question. Where relevant, name the page or section, attach a screenshot, or suggest replacement wording. Mark requested changes as **required before launch** or **can wait**.

## Content and credibility

### 5. Which statements or pieces of copy need correcting before you would approve them?

Consider accuracy, clarity, tone, service descriptions, and promises we can confidently make. Suggested replacement wording is welcome.

>

### 6. What information is missing elsewhere on the website that visitors need before they can take the next step?

Consider the questions you hear from customers or stakeholders that the site does not yet answer.

>

### 7. What approved evidence or assets should we add to make the website more credible?

Examples include project examples, testimonials, client logos, results, photos, or team biographies. If an asset is not yet approved for public use, flag that in your answer.
