# Diagram redesign research — 2026-09-28

The question: how to redraw every drawing on the site (the opening drawing, the
Workshops, Workflows and Automations drawings, the three audience scenes, the
playbook's review scene and the industry pages' portrait) as a new concept
rather than a restyle, keeping their wording, content, flow and colours exactly,
on a static GitHub Pages site. This note keeps **inspiration**, **facts** and
**judgments** apart. The decision it led to is
[ADR 0006](../docs/adr/0006-drawings-made-of-material.md).

**How this was done.** The handoff requires any subagent to run on
`gpt-6-luna` at `xhigh` effort and says to do the work in the main agent if that
combination is unavailable. It was not available in this environment, so all
research, design and implementation was done in the main agent. A planned
WebAssembly timing comparison was not completed: the Emscripten toolchain
install was stopped at the user's request, so this note makes no measured claim
about Wasm performance and relies on primary documentation for the platform
facts instead.

## 1. Inspiration

These informed the direction only. No image, wording or composition was copied
from any of them.

- **Josef Albers' paper exercises at the Bauhaus.** Students were set to make a
  structure from a single sheet of paper by cutting and folding it
  ([Getty Research Institute, "Albers Paper Exercises"](https://www.getty.edu/research/exhibitions_events/exhibitions/bauhaus/new_artist/matter_materials/interactive/);
  [bauhaus imaginista, "Open Your Eyes"](https://www.bauhaus-imaginista.org/articles/1703/open-your-eyes/)).
  Taken from it: a process can be _one piece of material_, and the fold is
  where the meaning is. The Workshops loop and the Workflows concertina are each
  one strip.
- **Bruno Munari's _Libri illeggibili_.** Books from 1949 on in which cut and
  folded coloured pages carry the story without text
  ([MassArt Artists' Books, "Libro Illeggibile N.Y. 1"](https://blogs.massart.edu/artistsbooks/2011/09/28/libro-illeggibile-n-y-1-by-bruno-munari/)).
  Taken from it: the material can carry the sequence, so the drawings show flow
  by what the paper does — plaited, fed through, stamped, threaded back — rather
  than by arrows.
- **Everyday processes that already mean what the content means.** A plait
  (separate things made into one, held by a tie), a mangle roller (crumpled in,
  flat out), a rubber stamp (a person's mark on an output), a leporello (panels
  read in order), a ribbon through a buckle and a ring (many held by one), a
  stair (a climb with a check at each step) and a gate. Each drawing takes the
  one whose behaviour matches its own flow; see the table in ADR 0006.
- **Ruled out as references:** nodes joined by lines, glowing or travelling
  signals, and the paper cards with pictograms of the reverted PR #64
  (`ae9d363`), which the brief excluded.

## 2. Facts

### What must not change

- `tests/fixtures/diagram-inventory.json` is generated by
  `scripts/diagram-inventory.mjs` from the deployed commit `40e42eb` by loading
  that commit's own modules. It records, for the four schematics, every node's
  words and kind and every connection in each orientation (with its weight:
  trunk, branch or return), and for the four scenes, every word with its kind and
  beat, every mark with its kind and beat, and the captions and descriptions.
- Two connections are loops, not steps: Workshops `n4>n1` (applied afterwards,
  back to the team's real work) in both orientations, and Workflows `n4>n2`
  (landscape) and `n4>n1` (portrait). Two drawings have unnamed outputs:
  the opening drawing (landscape only) and Automations (both), each
  `human>out1` and `human>out2`.

### Platform

- GitHub Pages: a published site may be no larger than 1 GB, has a soft
  bandwidth limit of 100 GB a month, and a deployment times out after 10 minutes
  ([GitHub Docs, "GitHub Pages limits"](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/github-pages-limits)).
- The deployed site sends no `Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy` or
  `Cross-Origin-Embedder-Policy` header (measured on 2026-09-28 with
  `curl -sI https://nicolas-found42.github.io/website-redesign/`: status 200,
  `server: GitHub.com`, `content-type`, `cache-control: max-age=600`, nothing
  else relevant).
- Without cross-origin isolation, the `SharedArrayBuffer` constructor is hidden
  and `postMessage()` throws for it; "your document must be in a secure context
  and cross-origin isolated"
  ([MDN, `SharedArrayBuffer` — Security requirements](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/JavaScript/Reference/Global_Objects/SharedArrayBuffer#security_requirements)).
  Threaded WebAssembly is therefore unavailable on this host without a
  service-worker workaround.

### Motion

- In the Web Animations model, an effect in its before phase (its delay) with a
  fill mode of `backwards` or `both` produces output from its first keyframe
  ([W3C, Web Animations Level 1, §4.8.3.1 "Calculating the active time"](https://www.w3.org/TR/web-animations-1/)).
  So a piece can wait for its beat already hidden, with nothing written to it.
- `Animation.cancel()` "clears all `KeyframeEffect`s caused by this animation
  and aborts its playback"
  ([MDN, `Animation.cancel()`](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Animation/cancel)).
  With nothing written to the element, cancelling an entrance leaves it in its
  own resting style.
- `transform-box: view-box` makes the nearest SVG viewport the reference box,
  positioned at the origin of the `viewBox` coordinate system
  ([W3C, CSS Transforms Level 1, §6](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-transforms-1/)),
  so a `transform-origin` in pixels is a point in the drawing's own units. That
  it resolves as written in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit is checked by
  `tests/system.spec.ts` ("the drawing's CSS geometry resolves the same in every
  engine").
- WCAG 2.2 success criterion 2.2.2 requires a way to pause, stop or hide moving
  content that starts automatically, lasts more than five seconds and is
  presented in parallel with other content
  ([W3C, Understanding SC 2.2.2](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide.html)).
  The longest new entrance, an audience scene with six beats, lasts about three
  seconds, and nothing repeats; the page's pause control remains.

## 3. Judgments

- **Keep SVG, real text and the Web Animations API.** The drawings are pictures
  assembled once, not simulations, so a GPU or Wasm renderer buys nothing and
  costs 14–150 KB plus the loss of prerendered no-script stills and of real,
  selectable, 13px-minimum text. The cross-origin-isolation fact above rules out
  threaded Wasm here anyway.
- **One object per drawing, not one kit restyled eight times.** A plait, a
  loop, a concertina, a stamp, a roller, ribbons through a ring, a stair and a
  set of gates. The shared kit is only materials — strips, folds, tags, seals,
  figures and six prints — so no two drawings share a shape.
- **Show flow by what the material does.** Strands plaited and tied; a strip
  that returns to where it started; a ribbon threaded back into the panel it
  refits; a stamp coming down on an output. No arrows and no lines between
  boxes.
- **Place pieces; never draw them.** An entrance is a piece arriving the way
  that object would — a sheet drops, a fold opens, a tag swings, a stamp
  presses — in the drawing's beats, then stillness. This keeps the beats the
  scenes already had and removes the "animated line" look entirely.
- **Keep colour meaning, not only colour values.** Red stays human; ink stays
  the result; each schematic source keeps one print (dots, stripes, rules), so
  a person following one strand across drawings sees the same material.
- **Hero: separate columns rather than a wash.** The collision is fixed by
  never setting text over the drawing. Between 861 and 1179px the drawing's
  column is narrow and tall, so it takes the portrait composition there.

### Open

- Input trunk/branch weights in the opening drawing are not shown by strand
  width, because a plait of unequal strands reads as a mistake. In landscape the
  trunk strand enters straight while the branches curve in; in portrait all
  three turn into the plait, so there the weight is not visible. The output
  trunk is a wider strip than any strand in both, and the landscape's two
  unnamed branches are narrow tails. The weights are kept in the content and
  the fixture.
- The Workshops loop is a rectangle and can read as a frame at a glance; its cut
  start and tucked end carry the direction.
- The opening drawing's portrait composition is taller than its landscape one.
