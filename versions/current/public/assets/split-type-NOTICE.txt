SplitType — attribution notice
==============================

The homepage bundles SplitType to measure the line boxes of the headings that
carry a masked entrance.

No licence text is reproduced here because the author has not published one.
The npm package ships no licence file, the repository contains no licence file,
and GitHub reports no detected licence for it; the single declaration of terms
anywhere is the `"license": "ISC"` field in the package manifest, checked on
2026-09-14. Writing out an ISC notice here would mean composing a copyright
line — a year and a holder — that the author has never asserted, so this notice
records the attribution and the declaration instead.

Confirming the terms with the author is recorded as pre-production work in
docs/LAUNCH-BACKLOG.md.

No SplitType source was copied into this project; it is used as a dependency
through its public API.
